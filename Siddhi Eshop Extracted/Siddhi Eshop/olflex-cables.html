Copper wire braid • EMC Screened
EMC Screened Tinned Copper Braided

Screened flexible control cable with tinned copper wire braid offering high electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) against interference in automation, PLC, and drive systems.

Screening: Tinned copper wire braid (CY) approx. 85% Outer Sheath: Special PVC, Transparent / Silver-grey Cores: Black with white numbers Primary Use: Plant engineering, industrial machinery, HVAC, conveyors, and EMC environments

4. ÖLFLEX® 100 I

PVC outer sheath • Colour coded as per IS 694 cores
BIS IS 694:2010 (ISI Marked) Unshielded • FR Only
