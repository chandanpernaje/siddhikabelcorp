About MENNEKES Germany

World Pioneer in Industrial Plugs & Receptacles

Founded in 1935 in Kirchhundem, Germany, MENNEKES Elektrotechnik GmbH & Co. KG is globally acknowledged as the gold standard in industrial electrical plugs and sockets, known under the trademark "MY POWER CONNECTION". With over 15,000 certified products sold in more than 90 countries, MENNEKES provides safe electrical power connections for demanding industrial environments including steel plants, mining, container ports, automotive factories, data centers, and construction sites.
