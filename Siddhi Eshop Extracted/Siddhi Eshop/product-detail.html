Product Description

Benefits

  • High electrical performance due to 4 kV test voltage
  • Oil-resistant according to DIN EN 50290-2-22 (TM54)
  • Space-saving installation due to small cable diameters
  • High flexibility thanks to fine-wire strand copper conductor construction

Application range

  • Plant engineering, Industrial machinery, Heating and air-conditioning systems
  • Machine tools, automated production lines, and conveyor systems
  • Mainly used in dry, damp and wet interiors (including water-oil mixtures)
  • For fixed installation as well as occasional flexing at free, non-continuously recurring movement without tensile load

Product features

  • Flame-retardant according IEC 60332-1-2
  • Good chemical resistance, see catalogue appendix T1
  • Oil-resistant according to DIN EN 50290-2-22 (TM54)

Norm references / Approvals

  • VDE reg. no. 7030
  • CE compliant according to Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU

Product Make-up

  • Fine-wire strand made of bare copper wires (Class 5 acc. IEC 60228)
  • PVC Insulation LAPP P8/1
  • Cores twisted in layers with optimal lay length
  • PVC outer sheath, silver-grey (RAL 7001)
